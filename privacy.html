Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 6, 2026

1. Who uses the app

AutomatisationN8N is a personal desktop tool used only by its owner, with TikTok accounts that the owner controls. It has no website accounts, no analytics and no advertising.

2. Data received from TikTok

  • user.info.basic: the open ID and display name of the connected account, used only to show which account is connected.
  • Access and refresh tokens: used only to upload videos as drafts (video.upload) and to check their upload status.

3. Storage

This data is stored only in local files on the owner's computer. It is never sent to any server other than TikTok's own API.

4. Sharing

No data is sold, shared with or disclosed to third parties.

5. Deletion

Access can be revoked at any time in the TikTok app (Settings and privacy > Security > Apps and services). Deleting the local token files removes all stored data.

6. Changes

This policy may be updated; the effective date above shows the latest version.
